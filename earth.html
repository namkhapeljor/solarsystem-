Earth

Our home. The only place we know of that has life, liquid oceans on the surface, and a protective atmosphere.

Earth Facts

  • Diameter: 12,742 km.
  • Distance from the Sun: about 149.6 million km.
  • One day: 24 hours. One year: 365.25 days.
  • Moons: 1.
  • Average surface temperature: about 15 °C.
  • About 71% of the surface is covered by water.
  • It is the densest planet in the solar system.
