Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest to the Sun. A scorched, cratered world with almost no atmosphere.

Mercury Facts

  • Diameter: 4,879 km, the smallest planet.
  • Distance from the Sun: about 57.9 million km.
  • One year: only 88 Earth days.
  • One day (sunrise to sunrise): about 176 Earth days.
  • Temperatures range from -180 °C to 430 °C.
  • Moons: 0.
  • Gravity is 38% of Earth's.
